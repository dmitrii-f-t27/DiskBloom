DiskBloom shows where your disk space went and helps you free it safely.

DISK MAP
• An interactive ring map of any folder or disk, up to six levels deep
• The largest folders and files at a glance, measured by the space they actually occupy
• Drill into any folder, see its full path and reveal it in Finder
• Queue items for cleanup and review the exact paths before anything moves

APP UNINSTALLER
• Removes an app together with its caches, preferences, saved state, logs and web data
• Builds only pre-approved exact paths from the app's bundle ID, with no fuzzy matching by default
• Checks the app's code signature before it pre-selects related data; shared and important data stay off until you choose them
• Protects running apps, Apple system apps and system locations

POSSIBLE LEFTOVERS
• Finds folders in your Library that belong to apps that are no longer installed
• Every candidate starts unselected and is checked again right before it moves

DUPLICATE FILES
• Finds files with identical content: size first, then a full SHA-256, then a byte-by-byte comparison
• Read-only: shows exact paths and lets you reveal every copy in Finder

CACHES
• See every cache, the app that owns it and a plain verdict: safe to clear, clear if you need space, quit the app first, or leave it
• Caches of running apps and iCloud or sign-in state cannot be selected

ASSISTANT
• Ask in plain words and it opens the right folder, cache list or app for you
• Uses Apple's on-device model, or any OpenAI-compatible API you choose
• It can select items, but you always review and confirm the move

SAFE BY DESIGN
• The disk tools run entirely on your Mac: no account, no analytics and no telemetry; only the optional assistant uses the API you choose
• Nothing is deleted permanently: after your review, items go to the Trash, where you can restore them
• DiskBloom reads only the folders you allow and remembers your choice
• Paths, identity and contents are verified again immediately before every move

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
